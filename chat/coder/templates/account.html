App icon

The icon on your home screen. After changing it, remove Dewdrop from your home screen and add it again (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen): your phone only reads the icon when you add it.

Password

Dewdrop only keeps a scrambled version of your password, so nobody can look it up, not even an admin. Let your password manager remember it: Safari offers to save it when you change it here. Forgot it? Sign in with a passkey and set a new one here.

At least 10 characters. Your other devices are signed out; this one stays signed in.

Passkeys

Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or your phone instead of typing your password. Your password keeps working as a backup.
