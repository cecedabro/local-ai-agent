Filling water, made to look real

Back to the filling drop. While the answer is written the water rises smoothly, slowing down as it gets fuller (it never claims "done"); when the answer is complete it drains and the drop is still again. The water is see-through: you see the drop's own light through it. Each option is shown big and at chat size; they loop: fill, hold, drain. Blossom uses the rose-quartz drop with pink water.
